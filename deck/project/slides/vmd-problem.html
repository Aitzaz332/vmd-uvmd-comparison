VMD: split a signal into K narrow-band modes

Constrained problem

min over {u_k}, {ω_k}

Σ_k ‖ ∂t[(δ(t) + j/(πt)) ∗ u_k(t)] · e^(−jω_k t) ‖²

subject to Σ_k u_k = f

Reading the terms

  • u_k: mode k, ω_k: its centre frequency
  • Hilbert term: makes u_k analytic, so its spectrum is one-sided
  • e^(−jω_k t): shifts mode k to baseband
  • ‖∂t ·‖²: the bandwidth of that shifted mode
  • Constraint: the modes must add up to f

Hyperparameters: number of modes K, bandwidth penalty α, dual step τ.

VMD minimises the summed bandwidth of K modes, each centred on its own frequency, under the constraint that the modes reconstruct the signal exactly.
