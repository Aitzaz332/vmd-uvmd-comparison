Further caveats

Other limits to keep in mind

Far from the fixed point

1 or 2 sweeps cannot reach what 500 reach, so the output is not a VMD solution

Non-causal

A global FFT sees the whole window, so forecasting use risks leakage from the future

Fixed window length

λ̂ has length 2T, so a different T needs new training

Batch size 1

One signal per step, so a GPU helps little

Training bookkeeping

Best weights are saved, then overwritten by the last epoch; loss still falling at epoch 100

Not tuned for forecasting

The paper reports that more layers overfit
