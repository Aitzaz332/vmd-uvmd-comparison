Caveat 2

The loss rewards reconstruction only

  • λ̂ is a free complex vector of length 2T: it can absorb the residual and cut the error without making better modes
  • α can collapse: with α → 0 a mode passes everything and the sum is exact
  • No term asks for orthogonal or narrow-band modes
  • One α and λ̂ for the whole dataset; changing T means retraining
