How separation is measured without ground truth

CriterionWhat it measuresBetter
Orthogonality indexΣ_{i<j} |⟨u_i, u_j⟩| / ‖x‖², content shared between modeslower
Spectral overlapShare of spectrum two modes occupy togetherlower
Relative bandwidthEnergy-weighted spectral width of a mode ÷ Nyquistlower
Energy ratioΣ‖u_k‖² / ‖x‖²; above 1 double counts, below 1 loses energycloser to 1
Effective modes1 / Σ s_k², s_k the energy share of mode k; a guard, not a scorenot near 1

On the synthetic data, where the true modes are known, relative bandwidth tracks true separation best (Spearman −0.85), orthogonality and overlap about −0.5. Reconstruction error points the wrong way (+0.52).

Spearman correlation against grouped mode SNR over 150 points (30 signals by 5 methods). The energy ratio showed no relation (-0.14).
