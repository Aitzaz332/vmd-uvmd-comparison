How the VMD loop runs

START

Initialise

ω_k on a uniform grid, û = 0, λ̂ = 0

→

STEP 1

Modes

Update û_k for k = 1..K, one after the other

→

STEP 2

Centres

ω_k from the mode’s power spectrum

→

STEP 3

Multiplier

Dual ascent on λ̂ with step τ

Repeat until the modes stop changing (tolerance) or the iteration cap is reached. Typically hundreds of FFT sweeps per signal.
