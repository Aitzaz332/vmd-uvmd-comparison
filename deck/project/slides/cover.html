Variational mode decomposition

From VMD to unfolded VMD

The ADMM solver, its deep-unfolded version UVMD (Ahmad et al., 2025), where it falls short in theory, and a three-dataset comparison at K = 13

Aitzaz · SPML group, Aarhus University · October 2026

Plan of the talk: classic VMD, the idea of unfolding, UVMD in detail, theoretical caveats, then reconstruction and separation results on synthetic, CWRU and LargeST data.
