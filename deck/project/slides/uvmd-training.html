How UVMD is trained

ItemSetting
LossSpectral reconstruction error, Σ |Σ_k û_k − f̂₊| (L1 in the code, L2 in the paper)
SupervisionNone: no ground-truth modes, the loss sees only the sum
OptimiserAdam, learning rate 0.1, batch size 1
ScheduleUp to 100 epochs, early stopping with patience 10
Data split70 / 15 / 15, random_state 42
Model sizeK = 13 modes, N = 1 or 2 layers
Initial valuesα_k = 2000, λ̂ = 0, ω on the uniform grid

The learned α and λ̂ are shared by every signal of the dataset; they are not computed per signal.

Our runs use the upstream training loop unchanged. The upstream code saves the best weights and then overwrites them with the last-epoch weights; the training loss was still falling at epoch 100.
